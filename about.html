About DCP

Family-Owned.

Since 1989, DCP has been helping food businesses succeed with packaging solutions that are reliable, innovative, and cost-effective. We believe strong relationships, exceptional service, and industry expertise are the foundation of every successful partnership.

Our Story

DCP is not just another plastic packaging manufacturer.

We provide high-quality thermoformed plastic packaging to customers across the country — more than 35 years in business, with decades of combined packaging expertise behind our team.

Started in Sparks, Nevada, DCP began with a simple mission: provide customers with the right packaging, at the right time, with the right service.

Today, we continue that mission by investing in our people, our technology, and our partnerships. We are creators of packaging together. We are your design experts. We are an extension of your brand.
