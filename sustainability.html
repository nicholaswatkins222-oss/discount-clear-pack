Making a Positive Impact

We partner with our customers to help achieve their sustainability goals while maintaining performance, quality, and food safety.

The majority of our clear containers are made from RPET (recycled polyethylene terephthalate) — one of the most widely accepted recyclable plastics in the U.S. The same crystal clarity that lets shoppers see your product is what allows RPET to be sorted, washed, and re-melted into new containers.

Every product in our catalog meets FDA food-contact material standards. No compromise on food safety — ever. The same material transparency we offer in our containers, we apply to sourcing.

Ocean container freight is the most carbon-efficient mode for high-volume shipments. Our supply chain leans on it by default, and U.S. warehouses positioned for regional fulfillment reduce last-mile transit.

Beyond Standard RPET

When your product or brand requires a different material, we source it.

Our supply chain isn't locked to a single plastic. Consumers, retailers, and brands are demanding more responsible packaging — DCP helps you meet that demand.

PLA (Plant-Based)

Polylactic acid — derived from corn or sugarcane. Compostable in industrial facilities. Best for brands marketing to sustainability-conscious shoppers.

PP (Polypropylene)

Higher heat tolerance than RPET. Used for hot-fill and microwave-safe applications. Widely accepted in #5 recycling streams.

100% Recycled Content

Higher post-consumer recycled content blends available on request. Talk to us about your sustainability claims and the PCR thresholds to match.
